Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 August 2026

Musical Match Saga (“we”, “us”, or “the App”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your choices.

Information We Collect

  • No personal account required: The game does not require you to sign in.
  • Local device storage: We store non-personal game progress and preferences locally (using localStorage), such as level progress and high score
  • Optional analytics: The App may include anonymous analytics to improve gameplay. Analytics (if enabled) collect non-identifying usage data such as levels played, session length, and feature usage. No personal identifiers (name, email) are collected by default.

How We Use Information

Data stored locally is used to restore your game state and preferences across sessions. Analytics data (if used) is aggregated to help us improve gameplay, balance levels, and prioritize fixes.

Third-Party Services

If you install the app through the Play Store or enable optional analytics, third-party providers (e.g., analytics platforms) may receive anonymized event data. Consult the app settings to opt out where provided.

Advertising

This app does not show personalised ads. If advertising is added in the future, it will be disclosed here and options to limit personalisation will be provided.

Your Choices

  • You can clear app data or uninstall the app to remove locally stored progress.
  • If analytics are present, there will be a setting to opt out of analytics collection in the app settings.

Contact

For privacy-related enquiries, please use the developer contact information provided on the app's Play Store listing.
