Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026

Musical Match Saga (“we”, “us”, or “the App”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your choices.

Information We Collect

  • No personal account required: The game does not require you to sign in.
  • Local device storage: We store non-personal game progress and preferences locally (using localStorage), such as level progress and high score
  • No analytics or tracking: The App does not currently include any analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. No usage data is collected or transmitted off your device. If this changes in a future update, this policy will be updated first.

How We Use Information

Data stored locally is used only to restore your game state and preferences across sessions on your own device. It is never transmitted anywhere.

Third-Party Services

The App does not share data with any third-party service. Distribution through the Play Store is handled by Google under its own privacy policy, which is separate from this one.

Advertising

This app does not show ads. If advertising is added in the future, it will be disclosed here and options to limit personalisation will be provided.

Your Choices

  • You can clear app data or uninstall the app to remove locally stored progress.

Contact

For privacy-related enquiries, please use the developer contact information provided on the app's Play Store listing.
