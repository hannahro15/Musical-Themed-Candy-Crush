Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

Musical Match Saga (“we”, “us”, or “the App”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your choices.

In short: this app collects no personal data and uses no analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs of any kind. Game progress and preferences are stored only locally on your device, and are never transmitted anywhere.

Information We Collect

  • No personal account required: The game does not require you to sign in.
  • Local device storage: We store non-personal game progress and preferences locally on your device (using localStorage), such as level progress, highest level reached, and high score.
  • No analytics or tracking: The App does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, or other usage tracking data.

How We Use Information

Data stored locally is used only to restore your game state and preferences across sessions on your device.

Third-Party Services

The app does not send gameplay or personal data to third-party analytics or advertising services. If you download the app from the Google Play Store, Google may process data according to its own policies for app delivery, updates, and store operations.

Advertising

This app does not display ads.

Your Choices

  • You can clear app data or uninstall the app to remove locally stored progress.

Contact

For privacy-related enquiries, please use the developer contact information provided on the app's Play Store listing.
